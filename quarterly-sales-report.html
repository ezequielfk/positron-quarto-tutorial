Quarterly totals

order_date is converted to a Date. Sales are then summed by calendar quarter, with the year included so that the same quarter in different years stays separate.

quarterly <- sales |>
       mutate(
              order_date = as.Date(order_date),
              quarter = floor_date(order_date, "quarter")
       ) |>
       summarise(total_sales = sum(sales, na.rm = TRUE), .by = quarter) |>
       arrange(quarter) |>
       mutate(label = paste0(year(quarter), " Q", quarter(quarter)))

The data covers 16 quarters, from 2015 Q1 to 2018 Q4.

Quarterly sales chart

ggplot(quarterly, aes(x = label, y = total_sales, group = 1)) +
  geom_col() +
  geom_line() +
  scale_y_continuous(labels = label_dollar()) +
  labs(x = "Quarter", y = "Total sales", title = "Quarterly retail sales") +
  theme(axis.text.x = element_text(angle = 45, hjust = 1))

In the chart, Q4 is the highest quarter in each year and Q1 is the lowest or close to it. Sales are higher overall in 2018 than in 2015.
